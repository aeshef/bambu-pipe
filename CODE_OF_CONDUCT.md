# Community conduct

This project welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience.
Ask questions, share reproducible failures and give feedback about the work.

- Be respectful. Discuss ideas and behavior without personal attacks.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, impersonate or reveal private information.
- Keep technical claims accurate. Distinguish measured results, assumptions and demos.
- Respect maintainers' and contributors' time. Avoid spam and repeated unsolicited promotion.
- Share minimal examples without credentials, printer access codes or personal data.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close abusive discussions and
restrict participation after repeated or serious violations. Use GitHub's
reporting tools for abuse. Do not publish private details while reporting it.

This is a small volunteer project; response times are best effort.
