# Desk-edge headphone hook

Design a one-piece prototype that slips over the edge of a **24 mm desk**.
Allow **0.5 mm** additional desk clearance. The extrusion width is **35 mm**,
the walls are **6 mm**, and the desk insertion depth is **38 mm**.

The hanging arm extends **42 mm** out from the back of the clip. Its retaining
tip rises **20 mm** above the arm's bottom. The desk slot begins **35 mm** above
the same bottom. Round the profile corners with a **2 mm** radius.

Add a **4.5 mm** through hole in the lower desk lip, **22 mm** from the back of
the desk slot. Add an underside hexagonal nut pocket: **7.2 mm across flats**
and **2.8 mm deep**, concentric with that hole. These are nominal dimensions
for optional M4 hardware; verify the particular nut and screw before printing.

Print the part on its side, with the 35 mm extrusion along Z and the bottom
at Z=0. The expected print-oriented envelope is **80 × 65.5 × 35 mm**.
Export editable Python, STEP, STL and all four inspection views. Check the desk
opening, screw hole, nut pocket and continuous load path independently.

Then adapt the same source to a **32 mm desk**. Keep the other dimensions:
the expected envelope becomes **80 × 73.5 × 35 mm**.

This is a CAD prototype. Fit, strength, hardware tolerances and slicer toolpaths
remain to be tested. No physical print is claimed.
